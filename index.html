How it works

Three steps, no scheduling

  1. STEP 1

    Employers post a job

    List as many jobs as you need and write custom questions for each one.

  2. STEP 2

    Interviewees show interest

    Interviewees browse jobs and say they are interested. Employers see the list and send invitations.

  3. STEP 3

    Round after round

    Each round is either a pre-recorded interview or a live one, and a live one can be recorded.

For interviewees

Record, keep and share your videos

Record in the studio while you watch yourself, with pause, stop and save, and redo. Videos stay on the site until you download them. Choose public or private, build playlists, and decide who can read comments.

For employers

Verified, organised hiring

Sign up, post jobs, see who is interested, and invite people to as many rounds as you like. Download or share any video with a link.
